Harvested in Our Backyard

From Our Trees to Your Table

At CW Farms, we tap maple trees right here in Brooten, Minnesota to collect pure sap each spring. Owned and operated by Chris Ward & Chris Welte, our small family farm has been producing small-batch maple and black walnut syrup using traditional methods. We collect sap in buckets, boil it down to perfection, and bottle every batch by hand.
