HARVESTED IN OUR BACKYARD

At CW Farms, we tap maple trees right here in Brooten, Minnesota to collect pure sap each spring. Owned and operated by Chris Ward & Chris Welte, our small family farm has been producing small-batch maple and black walnut syrup using traditional methods. We collect sap in buckets, boil it down to perfection, and bottle every batch by hand.

Our Process

From Sap to Syrup

Time-tested backyard methods that honor the craft.

1

Tapping the Trees

When the freeze-thaw cycle begins, we tap Sugar Maple and Black Walnut trees right here in Brooten.

2

Collecting the Sap

Fresh sap is gathered by hand in small batches to preserve quality and flavor.

3

Boiling & Bottling

We boil the sap down slowly over heat, then filter and bottle it fresh for your family.
